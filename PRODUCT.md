# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
ReactJS + Vite, React-Bootstrap, React Router (required by the course rubric). GSAP for motion (user request).

## Users
Fragrance enthusiasts ("fragrance heads") browsing for designer and niche scents. Inferred from the brief; not interviewed beyond it. A second audience is the course instructor, who grades against a fixed rubric.

## Product Purpose
DAT Fragrances is a hypothetical online fragrance storefront built as a class project by Deghne Agana, Theodore Navarro and Alexander Tolosa. It should feel premium, minimalist and made for people who care about notes, accords and houses.

## Positioning
A storefront curated by fragrance obsessives for fragrance obsessives. Product pages lead with the note pyramid, not generic retail copy. (Inferred from the brief.)

## Operating Context
Rubric pages: Home (CTA, featured products, button to a product page), Shop (at least 5 products), About (owners plus an origin paragraph), Contact (hypothetical contact and location info, plus a contact form), Product (description plus a no-op Add to Cart). Navigation: Home, Shop, About, Contact Us. Layout and color scheme must be consistent across pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Add to Cart does nothing (layout only).
- Prices are hypothetical, in PHP (₱).
- Products are real designer and niche fragrances (user choice): Creed Aventus, MFK Baccarat Rouge 540, Dior Sauvage Elixir, Tom Ford Oud Wood, Bleu de Chanel, Parfums de Marly Layton, Byredo Gypsy Water, Le Labo Santal 33.
- Descriptions, notes and prices are written by us and are not official brand claims.
- Footer states this is an academic, hypothetical store.

## Brand Commitments
Name: DAT Fragrances. Developed by Deghne Agana, Theodore Navarro and Alexander Tolosa. The user said "surprise me" on visual direction.

## Evidence on Hand
No real product photos. The user chose placeholder imagery. No testimonials, awards or real contact details. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Notes first: top, heart and base are the product's real information.
2. Minimal and quiet, with premium detail.
3. The path from browsing to a product page stays obvious.
4. Rubric compliance is non-negotiable.
